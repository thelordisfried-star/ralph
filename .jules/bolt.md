## 2025-02-20 - [Performance] ReactFlow Memoization and Referential Stability
**Learning:** ReactFlow is highly sensitive to the referential stability of props (like `fitViewOptions` and `deleteKeyCode`) and node components. Passing inline objects or failing to memoize custom node components (`React.memo`) causes excessive and unnecessary re-evaluations/re-renders of the graph internally, especially during interactions like panning or zooming.
**Action:** Always wrap `ReactFlow` custom nodes (`CustomNode`, `NoteNode`) in `React.memo` by default. Extract any static configuration objects or arrays passed to `<ReactFlow>` outside of the component body as module-level constants to ensure referential stability and optimize performance. Reuse memoized initial states (`initialNodes`, `initialEdges`) when resetting graph state to prevent O(N) recreations and memory allocations.
